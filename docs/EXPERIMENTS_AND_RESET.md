# Personal experiments and intentional reset

Personal → Personal growth → Experiments holds small trials with a question, action and editable start/end dates. A daily check-in saves whether the action was tried, an optional feeling from 1–5 and a note. One record per experiment/local date; dates outside the trial and future dates are rejected. Missing days never reset progress. Finish early or at the end with Keep, Adjust or Let go and a written conclusion. The app records personal observations, not causal or medical conclusions. Finished experiments retain their observations under What I learned.

Start fresh is available on each mode's overview and to-do list. It displays unfinished, non-archived tasks in that mode. Nothing is preselected. Resume removes postponement; Revisit later hides a task from daily focus and due-task reminders until its chosen local date; Archive hides it from active lists and calendar task deadlines without deletion. Original due dates stay stored. Postponed deadlines remain in the calendar. Subtasks are independent decisions, recurring instances are not generated by resetting, and completion history is never fabricated. Archived subtasks do not block completion of a retained parent.

The review step validates an atomic batch of up to 30 changes without committing it. Apply uses the same stable request key, with row versions to refuse stale edits. Undo is offered immediately and in Settings → ChatGPT & history → Saved changes for 30 days; it refuses to overwrite later edits. Later and Archived tabs provide an individual Resume action even after the undo window.

Design: extend the existing Personal palette with a small flask motif, elapsed-day fill, quiet save feedback and a settling leaf after reset; reduced-motion preferences suppress new animation.
